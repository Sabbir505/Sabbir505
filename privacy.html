Conduit Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Overview

Conduit is a desktop application that runs locally on your computer. This policy describes what information the app accesses and how it is handled. The app's developer does not operate any servers that receive your personal data.

Data that stays on your device

  • Chat history and settings are stored in a local database in your user profile folder.
  • Local AI models (GGUF files) run entirely on your machine — nothing is sent to a remote server.

Data accessed through optional integrations

If you connect an account (for example Google, GitHub, Notion or Canva), Conduit uses OAuth 2.0 to access that account only with your explicit consent. Access tokens are stored in your operating system's secure keychain on your device. The app communicates directly between your device and the third party (for example Google's APIs or the third party's MCP server); the developer does not see, store, or forward this data.

Online services you choose to use

When you chat with a cloud AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter), your messages are sent to that provider under its own privacy policy. Model updates are downloaded from the provider you select.

Data you generate

Artifacts (documents, PDFs, diagrams, code) you create are generated locally and saved to your computer or exported to destinations you choose.

Revoking access

You can disconnect any account at any time from Settings → Connectors, which revokes the token. You can also revoke access from the third party's own account settings.
